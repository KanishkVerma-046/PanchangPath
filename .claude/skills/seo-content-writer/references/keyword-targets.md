# Keyword Targets by Content Cluster

Seeded from real search-phrasing research (2026-07). Use this to ground Step 1 topic selection — pick from head terms and long-tail queries below rather than inventing keywords from scratch. Re-research a cluster if it starts to feel stale (new festival year, new calculator added, etc.).

## 1. Panchang & Tithi

**Head terms:** panchang, today's panchang, tithi calculator, nakshatra today, hindu calendar

**Long-tail / question queries:**
- "what is tithi in hindu calendar"
- "how to calculate tithi from date of birth"
- "difference between amanta and purnimanta calendar"
- "why does tithi change at different times"
- "what is rahu kalam and how is it calculated"

**Intent:** Mostly informational — readers want either today's exact value or to understand how the panchang system works. Good fit for explainer posts that also surface a live PanchangPath tool.

## 2. Festivals & Vrats

**Head terms:** hindu festivals 2026, ekadashi dates 2026, vrat katha, festival calendar

**Long-tail / question queries:**
- "ekadashi 2026 dates list"
- "why do we fast on ekadashi"
- "vrat katha meaning and significance"
- "which hindu festivals fall in [month] 2026"
- "difference between diwali and dhanteras"

**Intent:** Seasonal/calendar-driven — search volume spikes right before each festival. Time posts to publish 1-2 weeks ahead of the festival they cover.

## 3. Muhurta Timing

**Head terms:** shubh muhurat, griha pravesh muhurat 2026, choghadiya today, marriage muhurat

**Long-tail / question queries:**
- "best time for griha pravesh in 2026"
- "how to choose an auspicious wedding date"
- "what is choghadiya and how to read it"
- "which tithis are good for buying a vehicle"
- "what days of the week are avoided for muhurta"

**Intent:** High commercial-adjacent intent — readers are actively planning a real event (wedding, housewarming, purchase) and want a specific, actionable date/time, not just theory.

## 4. Jyotish & Kundali

**Head terms:** free kundli by date of birth, kundali matching, birth chart, mangal dosha

**Long-tail / question queries:**
- "how to read a birth chart / kundali"
- "what is kundali matching / kundali milan"
- "what is mangal dosha and how serious is it"
- "what is kalasarpa yoga"
- "how to find my rashi and nakshatra from date of birth"

**Intent:** Mix of tool-seeking ("free kundli") and explainer-seeking (what does a dosha actually mean). Strong internal-linking opportunity to PanchangPath's 15 Jyotish calculators.

## 5. Rashifal (Horoscope)

**Head terms:** aaj ka rashifal, daily horoscope, weekly rashifal, monthly rashifal

**Long-tail / question queries:**
- "aaj ka rashifal [rashi name]"
- "this week's horoscope for [rashi name]"
- "how accurate is daily rashifal"
- "difference between rashi and moon sign"

**Intent:** High-frequency, low-dwell-time searches. Best served by short, punchy explainer posts that link out to the live daily/weekly/monthly Rashifal pages rather than trying to replace them.

## 6. Devotional Practice

**Head terms:** hanuman chalisa lyrics, aarti sangrah, mantra meaning, chalisa collection

**Long-tail / question queries:**
- "hanuman chalisa lyrics with meaning"
- "how to do griha pravesh puja vidhi"
- "what is the significance of durga saptashati"
- "best time to recite gayatri mantra"
- "difference between aarti, chalisa, and stotram"

**Intent:** Reference-lookup and how-to. Strong internal-linking target — nearly every post in this cluster can link to a specific PanchangPath devotional detail page (e.g., `/devotional/chalisa/hanuman-chalisa`).
